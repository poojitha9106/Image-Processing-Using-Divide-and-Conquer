Algorithm Explanation

Divide: The image is divided into four smaller sub-regions at every recursive level.

Conquer: Each final region is processed independently using the selected filter.

Combine: The processed regions are placed back at their original coordinates to construct the final output image.

08

How the Application Works

The application follows the three major stages of the Divide and Conquer technique.

1. DIVIDE

01

The input image is recursively divided into 4 smaller sub-regions at each level up to the specified recursion depth.

2. CONQUER

02

Each leaf sub-region is processed independently with the chosen algorithm (Grayscale, Edge Detection, Sepia, Blur, etc.).

3. COMBINE

03

All processed sub-regions are combined back at their original relative coordinates to construct the complete output image.
